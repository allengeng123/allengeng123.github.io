Chuqin (Allen) Geng

I'm a final-year PhD student (visiting) at the University of Toronto, formally affiliated with McGill University and the Montreal Institute for Learning Algorithms (MILA), advised by Xujie Si. My research is about making neural networks explainable and verifiable — extracting interpretable logical rules from neural representations, and using them to reason about robustness and correctness.

Research

I'm interested in explainable AI and mechanistic interpretability, neuro-symbolic methods, and neural network robustness & verification. A lot of my work sits at the intersection of formal methods and deep learning — turning opaque neural representations into logical rules and specifications that can be inspected, verified, and trusted.
