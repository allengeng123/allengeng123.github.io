Research

I am broadly interested in neural network interpretability and neuro-symbolic methods, with the goal of building transparent and reliable AI systems. My work spans interpretable and reliable learning for graph, vision, and language models, as well as neural network robustness and formal verification. I currently study the mechanistic interpretability of LLMs and AI agents, tracing the representations and circuits behind their reasoning, planning, and decisions.
